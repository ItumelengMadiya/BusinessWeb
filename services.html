Our Services

Accounting & Payroll

We offer end-to-end accounting and payroll services for businesses of all sizes.

More Details
  • Bookkeeping: Regular tracking of financial transactions.
  • Payroll Management: Payroll processing, tax deductions, and compliance.
  • Financial Reporting: Profit & Loss statements, balance sheets, and cash flow reports.
  • Accounts Payable & Receivable: Ensuring timely payments and collections.

Tax Services

Our tax services include everything from tax returns to minimization strategies.

More Details
  • Tax Returns: Filing income tax, VAT, and capital gains tax returns.
  • Tax Planning: Strategies to minimize tax liabilities and optimize tax savings.
  • Compliance: Ensuring adherence to tax laws and meeting deadlines.
  • VAT & Tax Audits: Assistance with audits and regulatory reviews.

Company Registrations

We assist with the registration of companies, ensuring all documentation is prepared.

More Details
  • Business Formation: Comprehensive support for company registration, including name reservation and document submission.
  • SARS Registrations: Facilitation of VAT, PAYE, and UIF registrations to meet tax obligations.
  • CIPC Registration: Assistance with company name registration, structure setup, and annual return submissions to the Companies and Intellectual Property Commission.
  • COIDA Registration: Registration with COIDA to ensure employee coverage for work-related injuries and compliance guidance.
  • Legal Compliance: Ensuring adherence to all legal frameworks for smooth business operation.

Secretarial Services

Our secretarial services include company administrative support.

More Details
  • Corporate Governance: Ensuring your company follows corporate governance rules.
  • Annual Returns: Managing submissions of annual returns and compliance documents.
  • Board Meetings: Organizing, preparing agendas, taking minutes, and drafting resolutions.
  • Record Keeping: Maintaining statutory registers and updating company records.
